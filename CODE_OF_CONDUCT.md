# Code of Conduct

Pawmetry’s community welcomes people at every experience level. Treat others with respect, critique the work, and make room for different perspectives. Be specific and kind when offering feedback. Ask before sharing someone else’s personal details.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexual harassment, and publishing private information without permission are unacceptable. This applies to issues, pull requests, project discussions, and other spaces where someone officially represents Pawmetry.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the moderation contact that will be added before the repository opens. Include the relevant links or context you are comfortable sharing. The maintainer will limit disclosure to those who need the information to respond and may remove content, warn participants, or restrict access according to the severity and pattern of behavior. Retaliation against someone who reports a concern is unacceptable.
